# Roadmap

## Done

- Learnings from a topic; AI-proposed options for audience, level, time, 12 journey
  shapes, ways of learning, assessment styles, teacher personality, voice and face,
  plus topic-specific questions and free-text wishes.
- Journeys built from the choices and shown as a 3D world in the chosen shape (three.js):
  a growing tree with fruit, a mountain quest with a castle, floating islands, a bookshelf,
  a building going up, a spiral staircase, a neuron mind map, a concept web… Zoom, rotate,
  tap a step to fly to it. A plain list view is one tap away.
- **Seeing what the AI is doing:** options and journey steps appear live while the
  AI writes them (model, elapsed time, stage); lessons and chat replies stream word
  by word; voice lessons start speaking with the first sentence.
- **Motivation:** XP for real activity, levels, daily streaks, a weekly goal from the
  learner's sessions-per-week, 14 badges, confetti and level-up moments, a "continue"
  card, "you are here" / "just changed" markers, milestones and a history per learning.
- Ways of learning: reading, chat, voice, debate, practice, stories, question-led,
  **pictures and diagrams** (Mermaid diagrams + Wikimedia Commons photos),
  **narrated slideshows**, **face-to-face teacher** (a face the learner designs that
  talks), **games** (match, order, true/false, fill the gap) and **simulations**
  (choose-your-path role-play).
- Coach chat per learning that applies changes, with undo.
- Assessments: quiz, written, practical, scenarios, spoken, debate, flashcards,
  **picture/diagram questions**, **interactive challenges** (matching and ordering,
  marked exactly) and **spoken presentations**.
- Built-in Hugging Face models or the learner's own provider key and model.

## Next

- AI-generated images (e.g. FLUX on Hugging Face) as an opt-in, because they cost money.
- Real video (recorded or generated) instead of narrated slides.
- Reminders/notifications for streaks and weekly goals.
- Sharing a learning (e.g. a parent setting one up for a child).
- Progress insights across learnings.
